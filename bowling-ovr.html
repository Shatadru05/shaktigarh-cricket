Wicket-haul histogram (for IMP)

Empirical distribution

Enter the number of bowling innings with exactly 0, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, and 8+ wickets. All nine counts must add up to Bowling innings above. For older saved data with only a combined 5+ count, that count is carried into the 5-wicket field as a compatibility fallback; split it into 5, 6, 7 and 8+ if any 6+ hauls occurred.

Impact points per innings: 0 wickets = 0; 1 = 0; 2 = 0.25; 3 = 1.00; 4 = 1.60; 5 = 2.40; 6 = 3.40; 7 = 4.60; 8+ = 5.00. Raw impact rate = total haul points ÷ bowling innings. IMP is then converted against the editable target rate and target score below.

Overs use cricket notation: 12.3 means 12 overs and 3 balls, not 12.3 decimal overs. Dot-ball rate uses legal balls (overs × 6); boundary damage uses (4 × fours + 6 × sixes) per over. Enter consistent scorecard totals.

Starting assumptions — calibrate with match data

Scoring curves are editable calibration controls, not universal standards. IMP uses: target score + sensitivity × (raw impact rate − target raw impact rate). Defaults: raw impact rate 0.20 → IMP 80, with sensitivity 100; scores are capped to 0–100.
